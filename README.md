# 🌿 HerbSphere

An AI-powered business management platform for businesses in the Herbal & Aromatics sector.

## 📖 Overview

HerbAI Hub helps herbal product manufacturers, startups, and small businesses manage their products, customers, and orders through a centralized dashboard. The platform also leverages AI to generate marketing content and support business growth.

## 🚀 Features

- Product Management
  - Add, edit, organize, and manage herbal products.

- Customer Management
  - Maintain customer records and purchase history.

- Order Tracking
  - Monitor orders and manage sales workflows.

- Analytics Dashboard
  - Visualize business performance and sales trends.

- AI Marketing Content Generator
  - Generate product descriptions, promotional content, and social media captions using AI.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

### Frontend
- React.js
- Tailwind CSS

### Backend
- Node.js
- Express.js

### Database
- MongoDB

### Authentication
- JWT Authentication

### AI Integration
- Gemini API

### Deployment
- Vercel (Frontend)
- Render (Backend)

## 🎯 Target Users

Herbal and aromatics product manufacturers, startups, and small business owners looking for digital tools to streamline operations, improve customer engagement, and grow their online presence.
